Engineering Verse University Handbook

Academic Attendance Policy:
All undergraduate students must maintain a minimum attendance rate of 75% across registered courses. When a student's attendance drops below 75%, the department issues an academic warning and may require a meeting with the faculty advisor. Students with documented medical emergencies may request an attendance review through the academic office.

Hostel Admission Policy:
Students who wish to apply for a hostel room must complete the hostel application form, submit it to the residence office, and receive approval from the hostel warden. A refundable security deposit must be paid before final room allocation. Rooms are assigned based on availability and application priority.

Tuition Payment Policy:
Semester tuition must be paid before the published fee deadline. The university offers a short grace period of three working days for delayed transfers. After the grace period, a late fee is added automatically and the finance office may temporarily restrict access to student services until payment is completed.

Library Access Policy:
Each student receives a library account at the start of the semester. Books may be borrowed for fourteen days and renewed once unless another student has reserved the title. Overdue items attract a daily fine until they are returned or renewed.

Examination Registration Policy:
Students must complete examination registration through the student portal before the exam registration window closes. Registration is only considered final after academic dues and tuition dues are cleared.
